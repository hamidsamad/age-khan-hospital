Patient Feedback (Complaints, Appreciations and Suggestions)

At The Aga Khan University Hospital, our commitment lies in placing patients at the heart of our organization. We strive to deliver care that honours and addresses specific needs, and upholds personal values, ensuring that every clinical decision reflects patient priorities. Your feedback is invaluable to us and we prioritize it with utmost seriousness. Our dedicated medical and administrative teams are devoted to actively listening, comprehending, thoroughly investigating, and professionally addressing your complaints. Rest assured, sharing your thoughts with us will never impede your access to our services or the quality of care you receive. Instead, your input serves as a catalyst for continuous improvement across all aspects of our care delivery. So become a partner in our passion for continuous improvement and share your feedback.
